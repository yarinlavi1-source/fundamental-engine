# Financial and earnings-quality investigation

Reconcile income statement, balance sheet and cash flow before calling earnings cheap.
Collect annual and interim history with original units, currency, fiscal calendar,
accounting basis, continuing/discontinued scope and restatement lineage.

Check:
- Revenue recognition, deferred revenue, unbilled receivables, contract assets,
  returns/refunds and channel sell-in versus end-customer demand.
- Cash conversion over multiple periods. Working-capital timing is not automatically
  fraud, nor automatically harmless. Compare receivables/inventory to business drivers.
- Gross versus net revenue, acquired versus organic growth, FX and disposals.
- Capitalized development/software and depreciation lives; compare actual cash cost.
- SBC economic expense, current shares, weighted-average shares, options, convertibles
  and hypothetical financing. Avoid counting or excluding the same cost twice.
- Lease treatment, restricted cash, off-balance obligations, covenant/maturity profile.
- Management non-GAAP adjustments: reconcile every material adjustment to reported
  accounts; count its frequency and include unusual gains symmetrically.

Supported helpers are conservative. sec-periods extracts one exact concept and
can derive standalone quarters/TTM; it does not reconstruct every firm's statements.
The main financial_input is still annual. Never label the latest quarter as annual
or feed annualized guesses without a clearly separate forecast model.

A normalization is not a cash receipt or proof of future profitability. Keep reported,
comparable-scope and scenario views distinct. State the FCF definition explicitly,
especially lease principal, asset disposal proceeds and gross versus net capex.
