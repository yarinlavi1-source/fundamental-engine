# Portfolio context without changing company truth

Only use portfolio access available in the client, and only read what is necessary.
Keep account numbers, balances, positions and private documents out of public Git.
User-specific context is stored locally; public fixtures must be visibly synthetic.

After company research, distinguish business merit from portfolio suitability.
Examine issuer weight, overlapping economic exposures, common customers/suppliers,
industry/currency/geographic risks, liquidity, time horizon and dependence on the
same optimistic macro scenario. Sector labels can conceal correlated revenue drivers.

Do not treat a portfolio weight as evidence of a company's fair value. Do not invent
user risk preferences, tax assumptions or future contributions. If a personal
constraint is unknown, mark suitability unassessed and still complete company research.

Stress scenarios should state shocks and mechanisms, not unexplained correlations.
This repository has no trading/execution permissions and produces no automatic order.
The final output may identify overlapping risks or questions for the user, not
silently rebalance the account.
