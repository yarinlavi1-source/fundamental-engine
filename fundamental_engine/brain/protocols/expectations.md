# What does the price require?

Use the price of the correct security at the research cutoff. Reverse DCF solves
conditional operating assumptions, not a unique market consensus.
Run multiple combinations of margin, capital efficiency and discount rate; identical
prices can imply different growth-duration combinations. Reinvestment must move
with growth. Check whether implied volume/customer counts/capacity are feasible.

Separate three questions: is the business attractive, is the forecast plausible,
and is the existing stock price attractive under those assumptions?
Identify the operating achievement necessary to justify price, the evidence for it,
and what fraction of value depends on distant optionality.

For an earnings-event decline compare matched benchmark/peer returns, broader sector
news, rates, previous price run-up, prior expectations and other simultaneous news.
The repository's beta-adjusted residual is descriptive, not causal or statistically
significant. No quote/expectations data means no overreaction conclusion.

A re-rating is not a business cash flow. Do not count multiple expansion as both
higher terminal economics and a separate additional return without reconciliation.
