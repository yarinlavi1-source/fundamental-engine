# Research basis and theme-specific hypothesis maps — 2026-09-27

This packet guides questions, not stock recommendations. Economic chains below
are research hypotheses unless tied to dated company evidence in a dossier.

## General-purpose technology and complementary investment

Brynjolfsson, Rock and Syverson, The Productivity J-Curve (NBER w25148; published
AEJ: Macroeconomics 2021): https://www.nber.org/papers/w25148
The abstract describes how complementary intangible investment can precede measured
productivity gains. That motivates looking for adoption/implementation progress
before headline profitability. It does NOT imply every loss-making AI company is
investing wisely or will recover. Access note: search metadata/abstract available;
full PDF retrieval returned 403 in this session. No unseen result is relied on.

## Physical bottlenecks and alternative demand scenarios

IEA, Energy and AI, executive summary (2025):
https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/executive-summary
The report relates AI infrastructure growth to power, grids and deployment
constraints, with alternatives for adoption and efficiency. Local concentration
and construction times matter. Implementation: dated/scoped demand-supply cases,
substitutes and supply-response tests, rather than assuming a perpetual shortage.
AI demand -> power availability -> grid connection / equipment / cooling -> payer
-> specific qualified supplier. Check whether regulated returns, competition or
customer ownership prevent the supplier from capturing scarcity profit.

## AI security: risk growth is not automatically vendor revenue

NIST, Generative AI Profile, AI 600-1 (2024):
https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.600-1.pdf
NIST describes risks including information security and wider attack surfaces.
Implementation: test the link from a specific risk to operational responsibility,
procurement budget, deployment, renewal and incremental vendor revenue. A risk
framework is not a spending forecast or endorsement of a security stock.
Hypothesis map: autonomous agents -> machine identities/privileges/tool access ->
identity controls, observability, data controls and incident response. Alternatives:
existing vendors bundle the feature; open-source controls suffice; spending merely
shifts between vendors; AI reduces service cost and compresses vendor pricing.

## Connectivity / ALAB-type investigation

Astera Labs describes connectivity for rack-scale AI:
https://www.asteralabs.com/
Issuer marketing identifies products, not independent evidence of a moat or value.
Investigate compute density -> data movement / memory bandwidth -> qualified
interconnect solutions -> production demand and content per system. Verify protocol
changes, customer concentration, competitive silicon, insourcing, qualification
length and actual volumes after design wins. No claim here that ALAB is cheap or
that a specific future bottleneck is proven.

## AI cloud / NBIS-type investigation

Nebius describes GPU infrastructure and managed AI services:
https://nebius.com/
Investigate usable compute availability -> delivered capacity / reliability /
software experience -> customer acceptance, utilization and cash receipts. Separate
power agreements, funded construction, delivered hardware, active clusters and
recognized revenue. Test GPU obsolescence, capital intensity, competitive pricing,
customer concentration, prepayments and dilution. A provider benefiting from AI
can still deliver a poor shareholder return. No price target is inferred here.

## Enterprise implementation / PLTR-type investigation

As a research hypothesis, increasing model capability may move the scarce input
from models toward proprietary data access, permissions, workflow integration,
change management and measurable customer ROI. Test deployment conversion, cohort
expansion, repeatable implementations, delivery cost and cash contribution. The
name of a past winner is an analogy, never a matching algorithm or base-rate proof.

## Search beyond popular narratives

Generate adjacent hypotheses about advanced packaging, memory, thermal management,
networking, power quality, validation, identity, robotics deployment and data rights.
Choose at most three material hypotheses at a time. For each retrieve technical
standards or engineering papers, customer statements, supplier disclosures and
regulatory/grid/industry data. Forum posts and executive interviews suggest what
to investigate; primary evidence is needed for adopted technologies and contracts.
Record a dissenting engineering or commercial explanation, not just another bullish
article. Stop duplicating searches; preserve unknowns and set falsifiable milestones.

## Evaluation

Test that an unprofitable paid-adoption case remains discoverable, a design win is
not called revenue, famous endorsements cannot promote commercial stage, duplicated
news cannot inflate evidence breadth, future data is excluded, and contradictions
remain visible. These software tests measure neither winner detection nor returns.
Measure recall and false positives on frozen, held-out company cohorts INCLUDING
failures and delistings before making any predictive-accuracy claim.
