# Valuation research and implementation decisions (reviewed 2026-09-27)

This is a method bibliography, NOT issuer evidence. Original summaries below are
bounded; consult the source for the full treatment. No source guarantees investment
accuracy. Forum discussion is a source of questions, not a source of financial facts.

## Cash-flow claims and discount rates
CFA Institute, Free Cash Flow Valuation:
https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/free-cash-flow-valuation
Use FCFF with WACC and FCFE with cost of equity. Earnings/EBITDA cannot stand in
for investable cash. Forecast investment and working capital along with sales.
Implementation: terminal operating FCFF is valued with WACC; net claims are then
bridged into terminal equity. Dated dividends and terminal equity per share are
discounted with Ke. Funding/share issuance is explicit, so a financing gap is not
subtracted for a second time from the resulting owner value.

## Young firms and survival
Aswath Damodaran, Valuing Young, Start-up and Growth Companies (May 2009), 67 pages:
https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/pdfiles/papers/younggrowth.pdf
Forecast the economic path to maturity and reinvestment, not just the existing
financial ratios. Going-concern value is conditional on survival; failure and
recovery require a distinct case. The paper also illustrates changes in future
conditional value as a business develops. Implementation: explicit scenario paths,
funding gates and no uncalibrated default survival probability. A 2030 reporting
horizon does not require the business to become a perpetuity in 2030: extend the
explicit model to maturity and still display 2026–2030 dates.

## Economic profit and growth
McKinsey, Balancing ROIC and growth to build value (2006):
https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/strategy-and-corporate-finance/our-insights/balancing-roic-and-growth-to-build-value
Growth must be considered with the return generated by capital investment.
Implementation: stable-growth reinvestment rate equals g/ROIC, and growth cannot
exceed terminal ROIC or WACC. This is a consistency condition, not proof that a
chosen terminal ROIC is achievable.

## Banks and insurers
Damodaran, Financial service company characteristics:
https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/New_Home_Page/littlebook/financialsvccompanies.htm
For financial firms, debt is often part of operations, and regulatory capital
constrains expansion. Implementation: dedicated equity route and capital checks.
CFA Institute, Residual Income Valuation:
https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/residual-income-valuation
Book equity and earnings above the equity charge provide an alternative to cash
flow valuation. Implementation: clean-surplus book rollforward, residual income,
and terminal B*(ROE-g)/(Ke-g). Current implementation assumes constant shares,
no OCI adjustments and a reconciled regulatory book. It is not a full insurance
reserve, solvency, ALM or recapitalization model.

## Cycles and recovery
Damodaran, More on normalizing earnings:
https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/New_Home_Page/valquestions/normearn.htm
Normalize across a full cycle and account for changes in company scale. A gradual
recovery must not be valued as if normalization happened immediately. Implementation:
period-specific recovery assumptions, a structural-downside alternative, and no
automatic removal of recurring restructuring or stock compensation costs.

## REIT cash generation and asset value
Nareit Financial Standards White Paper, 2018 restatement, 9 pages:
https://www.reit.com/sites/default/files/2018-FFO-white-paper-(11-27-18).pdf
Nareit FFO is a supplemental operating-performance definition for REITs, not a
universal substitute for cash available to shareholders. Implementation: property
NAV is explicit; maintenance, tenant improvements, leasing commissions and other
AFFO adjustments require reconciliation before a cash-flow cross-check.

## Clinical stage base rates
BIO / Informa / QLS, Clinical Development Success Rates 2011–2020, 34 pages:
https://go.bio.org/rs/490-EHZ-999/images/ClinicalDevelopmentSuccessRates2011_2020.pdf
The study tracks clinical/regulatory transitions across programs and examines
variation across disease and program characteristics. Implementation: analyst
supplies dated, stage-appropriate probabilities and their source/cohort match;
there is no single baked-in approval rate. Development costs and commercial
receipts can have different probabilities of occurrence. Correlated programs
and financing still need separate analysis.

## Validation standard
Unit tests validate selected arithmetic and rejection rules. Forecast accuracy
requires frozen pre-outcome cases, later actuals, matched definitions, survivorship
controls and held-out time periods. forecast_score reports descriptive errors and
interval coverage, never a stock-selection win rate. No accuracy claim is made.
