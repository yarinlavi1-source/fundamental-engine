# Evidence ledger and competing claims

A fact requires a reviewed observation of an actual outcome. Guidance is a statement
about an intended future; documenting it proves the statement was made, not achieved.
An inference connects observations through a stated causal mechanism. An assumption
is a chosen scenario input. Label all four explicitly.

For every critical/high claim record: statement, dimension, materiality, kind,
supporting observation IDs, challenging observation IDs, reviewed_at and falsifier.
Ask: what exactly does this observation establish, and what does it NOT establish?
A customer-logo slide does not establish revenue, exclusivity or successful adoption.
Backlog does not prove revenue timing, profitability or collectibility.

Prefer original filings/notes, original transcripts and direct operational evidence.
Company publications can establish reported numbers but are interested parties when
explaining causes. Competitor/regulator/customer evidence may test attribution.
A creator such as כלכלה מאפס can supply a thesis or a lead: preserve authorship,
date, original evidence and conflicts of interest; verify material numeric claims.
Do not transform an opinion or disclosed trade into the company's financial fact.

Give syndicated documents the same origin_id. Preserve exact-copy hashes when
available. Several independent domains can still repeat one press release.
Independent origin is not necessarily independent methodology.

Conflicting numbers: first compare issuer, scope, dates, units, GAAP/non-GAAP,
continued operations, restatement version, gross/net presentation and share class.
Do not average unlike definitions. Preserve both values and the reason one is chosen.
A corrected disclosure may supersede an earlier one only AFTER its availability date.

Quote short relevant passages with locations where permitted; do not copy entire
copyrighted publications into public examples. Raw private documents stay local.
