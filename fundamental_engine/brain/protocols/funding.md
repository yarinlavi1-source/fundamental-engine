# Financing and value retained by the current share

Identify cash available for operations (exclude restricted/required balances),
burn, working-capital needs, capex schedule, debt maturities, covenants and contingencies.
Distinguish binding committed facilities from management intent and hypothetical access.
Do not assume all securities reported as cash equivalents can fund every subsidiary.

Use ownership_valuation for financing-sensitive nonfinancial businesses.
Specify time, instrument, gross proceeds, fees, issue price, interest, repayments
and share count. Compare with the no-hypothetical-financing route. Make affordable
versus severely dilutive financing distinct outcomes. Never use the present stock
price as a guaranteed future issuance price.

Capital intensity follows growth. A big revenue forecast with fixed reinvestment
is usually not a coherent funding case. Show downside adoption combined with
worse issuance prices; a standalone sensitivity can miss interacting risks.

The implemented annual model assumes financing at year start, cash-equivalent SBC,
zero return on retained cash and a terminal business sale. It does not detect
within-year shortfalls, model employee options exactly or verify debt capacity.
If these dominate, return a research/model limitation rather than a precise number.
A funding gap has unknown recovery value, not an automatic zero or automatic bailout.
