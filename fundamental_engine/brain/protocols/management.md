# Management and capital allocation

Track original promises, exact metric definitions, stated period, source and eventual
outcome. Keep amended guidance alongside the original rather than replacing it.
A comparison is valid only for the same scope, accounting basis, units and period.
Publication may follow the fiscal deadline. Missing outcomes are unknown, not misses.

Evaluate capital allocation against alternatives: organic reinvestment, acquisition,
debt repayment, dividends and buybacks. Examine per-share outcomes after SBC and
financing. Acquisition revenue can hide weak organic performance; accretive EPS alone
is not proof of value creation. Buybacks at an excessive price can destroy value.

Check incentives, related-party transactions, insider ownership, control structure,
compensation targets and whether adjusted metrics exclude recurring economic costs.
Distinguish integrity allegations from documented governance facts. Do not infer
managerial dishonesty from one forecast miss or investor charisma from business skill.

The management_promises ledger computes matched numeric outcomes; it does not give
a universal management score. Record the coverage and omitted promises so selective
memory cannot make every manager appear prescient or incompetent.
