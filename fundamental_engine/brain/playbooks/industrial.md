# Industrial and cyclical businesses

Decompose revenue into volume, realized price, mix, acquisitions and FX. Understand
capacity, utilization, order cycle, dealer/customer inventory, backlog quality,
cancellations and conversion into deliveries/cash. Track maintenance versus expansion
capital and the working capital consumed by recovery.

Use mid-cycle earning power with evidence from more than one cycle where available.
A low multiple at peak utilization may be expensive. A weak quarter during planned
maintenance may be temporary, but future unit cost and customer loss still matter.

For project businesses: contract type, fixed-price exposure, inflation escalators,
performance guarantees, provisions, progress payments and cash timing. Backlog can
contain economically harmful work. For manufacturing: yield, scrap, warranty, labor,
energy and supplier bottlenecks. Restarting production is not restored throughput.

Test temporary disruption against repeated cost overruns, competitor share gains,
customer substitution and contractual remedies. Avoid booking lost output as sales.
Forecast delivery capacity before revenue growth; expansion capex cannot produce
instantaneous utilization. Funding and covenant headroom matter during cycle troughs.
