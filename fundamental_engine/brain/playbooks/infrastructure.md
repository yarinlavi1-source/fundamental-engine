# Infrastructure, data centers and capital-heavy growth

Map operating capacity × utilization × realized contract price. Separate announced,
permitted, financed, built, energized and revenue-generating capacity. MW, racks,
compute units and contracted revenue are not interchangeable.

Inspect customer credit, concentration, minimum commitments, termination rights,
escalators, deposit terms, contract duration and remaining performance obligations.
A marquee customer can validate demand but also dictate economics and refinancing.

Model construction schedule, power interconnection, land/lease cost, hardware refresh,
maintenance, efficiency, operating service costs and working-capital timing. Hardware
obsolescence and residual value can dominate apparent project margins.

For each expansion milestone record capital spent before revenue, delay sensitivity,
funding source and current equity dilution. Test simultaneous late commissioning,
lower utilization and worse financing terms. Do not count conditional financing as
cash or announced capacity as operating assets.

The current ownership model is annual and simplified. Project-finance waterfalls,
covenant restrictions and within-year liquidity can require dedicated modeling.
Mark those limits rather than presenting a precise project value from generic DCF.
