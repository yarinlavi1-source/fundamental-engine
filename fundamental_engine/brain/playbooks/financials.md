# Banks and insurers — dedicated valuation required

Research can proceed; generic industrial FCFF is not authorized for these economics.
Debt/funding and regulatory capital are part of the operating business. Use suitable
book-value/residual-income/dividend or insurance valuation only when implemented and
reviewed; the current calculator must withhold a priced conclusion.

Banks: deposit mix and concentration, funding cost, asset/liability repricing,
duration and unrealized losses, credit vintages, provisions versus realized losses,
nonperforming assets, reserve coverage and capital requirements. Compare normalized
returns on equity against cost of equity and required capital retention.

Insurers: underwriting cohorts, combined ratio definition, reserve development,
catastrophe exposure/reinsurance, investment book risk and capital adequacy. Favorable
reserve releases may temporarily flatter earnings; premium growth can mean poor pricing.

Distinguish liquidity, solvency and profitability. A low price/book ratio does not
establish undervaluation when book value or earning power is uncertain. Record the
specialist model/data requirement explicitly; do not relabel the sector to pass gates.


## Hybrid digital banks and fintech (SOFI lesson)

Separate banking/lending, fee services and technology economics using issuer
segment definitions; reconcile intersegment allocations and shared costs. Do not
count loan-platform fees in both lending and financial services. Contribution
profit, operating profit, adjusted EBITDA and GAAP net income are distinct.
Compare consistent fiscal periods and GAAP/adjusted EPS bases across peers.
Do not apply software Rule of 40 or a technology multiple as a bank quality gate.

Test credit vintages, delinquent-loan sales, normalized loss rates, deposit pricing
and retention, funding concentration, capital retention and incremental ROTCE
(return on tangible common equity, not generic ROE). Fees may be capital-light
but are not automatically credit-risk-free; examine recourse and commitments.
A deposit license helps relative to wholesale-funded lenders, not automatically
relative to other licensed banks. Deposits are liabilities, not surplus equity.

Use residual-income/dividend/FCFE models with regulatory reinvestment or reviewed
segment valuation. Future EPS x exit multiple is a dated sensitivity, not current
fair value until discounted with an appropriate cost of equity. Check capped-call
ceilings, dilution, and the financial cost of hedges; never call them cost-free.
Verify regulatory proposal vs final rule, effective date, scope and affected firms
against the regulator. Institutional passive ownership is not an active endorsement;
insider purchases and short interest alone do not establish value or price direction.
