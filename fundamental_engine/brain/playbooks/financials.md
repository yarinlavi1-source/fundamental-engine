# Banks and insurers — dedicated valuation required

Research can proceed; generic industrial FCFF is not authorized for these economics.
Debt/funding and regulatory capital are part of the operating business. Use suitable
book-value/residual-income/dividend or insurance valuation only when implemented and
reviewed; the current calculator must withhold a priced conclusion.

Banks: deposit mix and concentration, funding cost, asset/liability repricing,
duration and unrealized losses, credit vintages, provisions versus realized losses,
nonperforming assets, reserve coverage and capital requirements. Compare normalized
returns on equity against cost of equity and required capital retention.

Insurers: underwriting cohorts, combined ratio definition, reserve development,
catastrophe exposure/reinsurance, investment book risk and capital adequacy. Favorable
reserve releases may temporarily flatter earnings; premium growth can mean poor pricing.

Distinguish liquidity, solvency and profitability. A low price/book ratio does not
establish undervaluation when book value or earning power is uncertain. Record the
specialist model/data requirement explicitly; do not relabel the sector to pass gates.
