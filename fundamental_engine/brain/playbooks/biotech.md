# Biotech and clinical optionality — specialist model required

Identify asset, indication, trial phase, control group, endpoint, statistical plan,
safety profile, enrollment, competing standard of care and regulatory path. A press
release describing a positive result is not a substitute for the actual endpoint,
effect size, limitations and complete trial evidence.

Commercial potential requires addressable treated population, eligible share,
reimbursement, net price, manufacturing, distribution and time. Scientific success
can coexist with unattractive shareholder return after funding and dilution.

Separate programs and stage-specific costs. Track cash runway through the next
readout and commercialization, not merely to the next quarter. Milestones require
exact success/failure criteria; do not invent approval probabilities from enthusiasm.

The current financial engine lacks a validated clinical risk-adjusted asset model.
Produce a structured evidence/financing assessment and withhold generic perpetuity
valuation for binary pre-revenue programs. Unknown expert interpretation should be
identified, not replaced by a confident language-model extrapolation.
