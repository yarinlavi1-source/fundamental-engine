# Conglomerates and multiple economic engines

Map each material segment's customer, business model, capital needs and earnings
quality. Explain eliminations, intercompany transfers, shared costs, financing,
minority interests, holding-company claims and any double counting.

One consolidated growth rate can conceal a shrinking legacy business and a small
rapidly growing operation. Value-driving optionality must be material enough relative
to the group and owned by the listed equity, not just economically associated with it.

Use appropriate segment methods and reconcile to consolidated enterprise/equity
claims. Avoid valuing a subsidiary fully while also capitalizing its dividends or
including it again in book assets. Corporate costs and tax leakage need explicit
allocation. Conglomerate discounts are not an automatic bonus return.

The current calculator does not implement audited SOTP/NAV reconciliation. Qualitative
segment research is supported; a priced conclusion must remain withheld until a
suitable model is executed. Use multiple playbooks for the individual economics.
