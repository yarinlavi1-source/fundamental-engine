## Caveats

- survivorship: the universe is today's largest US companies, so every realized price is a survivor's, and the cross-section table is survivor-biased by construction; the deflated check (6.8) rescales each window to SPY and is reported next to the raw results, not instead of them
- in-sample design: v2's changes were chosen from v1's 2016-2025 attribution (section 1 / 8 of the preregistration); the constants were stated with reasons and fixed before this run, not searched, but the period is not out of sample
- no guidance or consensus in the backtest: the run-rate + base-rate variant only; guided paths are untested
- one formation a year: yearly rank statistics with t below about 2 are noise
- the attribution conditions on a usable row (NI > 0) at t + h; it locates the error, it does not validate a fix
- the engine_irr baseline comes from the engine walk-forward rows supplied (scripts/backtest_engine.py); lane_v1 from the spec-1 rows supplied (deterministic re-run of the unchanged spec-1 code)
